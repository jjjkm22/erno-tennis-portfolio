About Erno

A lifetime on the court.
A global perspective.

Tennis has been part of Erno's life since childhood. His passion for the game has grown into a commitment to helping players learn, improve and enjoy every moment on court.

After coaching children in Australia and New Zealand, Erno now works at Smash Espoo in Finland and also offers private lessons. His international background brings a broad perspective and an adaptable approach to coaching.
